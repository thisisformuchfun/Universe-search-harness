# Original 14 held objects — imaging addendum

All 14 remain held. Two are previously published compact-binary candidates and are controls, never discoveries. Novelty for the other 12 remains unresolved. The [previous source-by-source matrix](../held14-rv-recovery-20261102T0622Z-01a193cc/SOURCE_MATRIX.md) and [literature queue](../zhou-mrt-20261102T1041Z-01a193cc/REVIEW_QUEUE_V5.md) retain the independent RV, luminous-companion, blend, triple and scan-angle risks for every object.

For Gaia DR3 **1533365584518328576**, a separately controlled [Legacy DR9-north cutout](RUN_REPORT.md) covers the Gaia and one-detection PS1 positions in g/r/z. At the target and at a brighter Gaia neighbor 20.28278 arcsec away, the 5x5 z-band inverse-variance windows are entirely zero; 5 of 25 pixels at the PS1-offset window also have zero z weight. The two central image windows are close to 10, but the cause of zero weights is unknown. All 5x5 g/r weights at the three locations are positive, which is not a quality mask, saturation test, PSF calibration or source detection. The nearer PS1 source remains unconfirmed, and the LAMOST fibre attribution and orbit remain unresolved. See the [reproducible counts](LOCAL_WEIGHT_COUNTS.json), [FITS inventory](TARGET_FITS_INVENTORY.json), [independent science review](INDEPENDENT_SCIENCE_FITS_REVIEW.md), and [transport review](INDEPENDENT_EXECUTION_CAPTURE_REVIEW.md).

The useful next steps are source-attributed, phase-separated RV observations and validated resolved-light or mask/PSF information. A possible public DR9 maskbits product and a different SDSS DR17 optical-catalogue route are leads, not completed checks. No scientific criterion, release policy, ranking or holdout was changed.

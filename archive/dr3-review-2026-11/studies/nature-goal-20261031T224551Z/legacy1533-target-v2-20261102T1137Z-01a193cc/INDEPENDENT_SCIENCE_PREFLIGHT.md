# Independent science review of proposed target field

On 2026-11-02 UTC `/root/ps1_science_review` accepted the proposed 192-pixel DR9-north g/r/z subimage request as descriptive feasibility only. At 0.262 arcsec/pixel its nominal 50.304-arcsec field includes a previously noted brighter field source approximately 19.9 arcsec north, subject to actual WCS and coverage. That source is not yet a validated PSF control: saturation, isolation, masking, wings and epoch propagation remain unknown. A 4 MiB cap is reasonable but overlapping brick products can exceed it; a cap stop is unavailable, not a non-detection.

The target must be checked separately for per-band image/weight pairs, its own WCS, positive weights, finite coverage and metadata. The reviewer cautions that the control says `SURVEY=DECaLS` and `VERSION=DR9-north`; the provider's public viewer code sets DECaLS as a generic cutout header literal, but deployed code and contributing exposures were not verified. No RADESYS or EQUINOX card appears in the control, and Astropy's ICRS default is not an independent astrometric calibration. No detection threshold, exclusion, orbit, discovery, release criterion, ranking or holdout changes are authorized by this metadata/image request.

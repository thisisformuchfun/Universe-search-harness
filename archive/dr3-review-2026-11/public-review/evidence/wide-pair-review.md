# Published wide-pair association verified

The returned VizieR row contains exact `long` identifiers `2852726284550996352` and `2852726284550996224`. Its schema defines the separation as degrees and projected separation as AU: the values are 6.0894844 arcsec and 5637.7546 AU. The reported `R=0.0034303636` and `BinType=MSMS` were independently read from the immutable XML; no floating-point conversion of either identity occurred.

In the [primary paper, §3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2101.05282v3#S3.SS2), R is the seven-dimensional KDE density of shifted-catalogue chance alignments divided by candidate density. It approximates a chance-alignment probability but is explicitly not strictly a probability. This small value supports the published likely-wide-binary association; it is not a calibrated BH or triple probability. The paper's MS category separates objects from white dwarfs on the colour–magnitude diagram and includes evolved non-WD stars. MSMS is not a spectroscopically established pair of unevolved main-sequence stars. The catalogue removes resolved triples and its selection can also omit unresolved higher multiples, so catalogue absence would not exclude such configurations.

Our interpretation: this upgrades the former neighbour lead to a **previously published wide stellar-pair association**. It does not validate the target's separate short-period Gaia NSS solution or show that the wide star caused a blend. A hierarchical triple requires an independently established inner system; a BH requires additional dynamical and stellar evidence. The catalogue uses Gaia EDR3 astrometry, so it is not an independent measurement replication of the DR3 NSS orbit. Publication of a wide stellar association alone also does not establish prior compact-companion candidacy.

The successful Source1 row remains positive evidence even though the complementary Source2 query timed out. No negative result for that second query or exhaustive membership claim is made. Root's separate current-Gaia context found no independent epoch RV series for this pair; a combined-CCF RV summary cannot supply a same-instrument difference test.

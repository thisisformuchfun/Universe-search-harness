# Public literature and cross-identification review

[`review.json`](review.json) records the dated per-source checks and public contact links. The independently checkable inputs are in [`evidence/`](evidence/) with saved capture metadata where available. They were obtained from public services between 2026-10-31 and 2026-11-02. Request/capture JSON may include the original local paths; HTTP headers that might contain session information are not included.

The two published compact-candidate IDs are `4491401821468887552` and `824344003814438528`, in [Li et al. 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34715). Neither is claimed as a confirmed black hole. The other 12 are not cleared for novelty. [Simon et al. 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20371), the two [Müller-Horn et al. parent catalogues](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17271785), and the published [Zhou & Gu tables](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/aeaa3a) were checked only over their stated finite samples. Li's full 1,835-row table was unavailable. A search index missed known-positive controls; a web-search miss is not evidence of absence.

Eight objects occur in the [Li et al. 2025 XP parent dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/15166185); a spectral-model class is not a black-hole or confirmed-binary classification. The complete XP source-ID column and selected byte ranges were checked, but the checksum of the entire 9.86 GB HDF5 file was not verified or bundled. One object occurs in the [El-Badry et al. 2021 wide-pair catalog](https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/J/MNRAS/506/2269); this is not evidence of an inner black-hole orbit. Two public LAMOST spectra and 14 Gaia-to-2MASS cross-identifications are recorded; one cross-identification may be shared.

Run `python -B verify_public_review.py` to recompute the finite membership checks from included evidence and compare the saved dashboard rows. This does not perform network access or change scientific dispositions. The Zhou Type I check and XP / wide-pair conclusions are based on separately saved review records, not a fresh download of their complete catalogs.

Johanna Müller-Horn, Hans-Walter Rix, Kareem El-Badry, Ben Pennell, Matthew Green, Jiadong Li, and Rhys Seeburger, *Dormant black hole candidates from Gaia DR3 summary diagnostics — supplementary material*, Zenodo record 19181131, version 2: https://zenodo.org/records/19181131.

# Original 14 held objects — evidence addendum

All 14 original anomalies remain held. Gaia DR3 `4491401821468887552` and
`824344003814438528` appear as previously published compact-binary candidates
in [Li et al. (2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.34715v1), so they are
literature controls, never discoveries. The other 12 have unresolved novelty;
absence from a checked list is not proof of novelty. No new independently
validated orbit or black hole was established.

The [source-by-source matrix](../held14-rv-recovery-20261102T0622Z-01a193cc/SOURCE_MATRIX.md)
and [previous image queue](../held1533-ps1-image-20261102T0648Z-01a193cc/REVIEW_QUEUE_V3.md)
remain the basis for all 14 source-specific risks, including luminous
companions, blends, triples, scan-angle aliases and needed measurements.

For Gaia DR3 `1533365584518328576`, a public SEDR5 product matches the saved
DR6 plate/fibre observation metadata, but Gaia light attribution, retained
contributors and independent RV calibration remain unresolved. The
[filename query](../sedr5-target-query-20261102T0913Z-01a193cc/RUN_REPORT.md)
and [`specid` query](../sedr5-specid-query-20261102T0919Z-01a193cc/RUN_REPORT.md)
returned HTTP 200 with empty bodies after positive controls; neither is a
validated nonmatch or an RV measurement. The [identifier clarification](../sedr5-specid-query-20261102T0919Z-01a193cc/IDENTIFIER_CLARIFICATION.md)
distinguishes `specid` from `obsid`.

The [pixel inventory](../sedr5-pixel-feasibility-v2-20261102T0928Z-01a193cc/RUN_REPORT.md),
[paired-array prototype](../sedr5-pixel-orientation-v2-20261102T0935Z-01a193cc/RUN_REPORT.md)
and [different-observation reference](RUN_REPORT.md) document numerical

They do not establish calibration, physical alignment, usable RVs or an orbit.
